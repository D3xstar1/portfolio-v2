    Process

    From first call to launch

    1. 01

      Plan

      We talk through your business, your customers and what the site needs to do. You get a written plan and a fixed quote.

      About 1 week
    2. 02

      Design

      I design the key pages and share them with you. We adjust until they feel right, before any code is written.

      1–2 weeks
    3. 03

      Build

      I build the site on a private preview link so you can click through it and send feedback as it comes together.

      2–4 weeks
    4. 04

      Launch

      I connect your domain, set up hosting and email, and show you how to edit your own content.

      Launch day, then support
